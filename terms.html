Al Falah Charity Organization Policies

Guiding principles for our charitable work and community engagement

Contribution Policy

Guidelines for contributing to Al Falah Charity Organization's mission and programs

  • 1. Purpose of Contributions: All contributions (financial, in-kind, or volunteer services) must align with our mission to serve communities in need and comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
  • 2. Financial Contributions:
    • Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law
    • We issue official receipts for all monetary donations
    • Funds are allocated according to donor intent when specified
  • 3. In-Kind Donations: We accept physical goods that directly support our programs. All items must be new or gently used and appropriate for distribution to beneficiaries.
  • 4. Volunteer Contributions: Volunteer services must comply with our code of conduct and safety guidelines. We reserve the right to conduct background checks when appropriate.
  • 5. Restricted Items: We cannot accept: weapons, expired items, recalled products, damaged goods, or any items that violate our ethical standards.
  • 6. Transparency: We maintain accurate records of all contributions and use them solely for charitable purposes as registered with relevant authorities.
  • 7. Right to Refuse: We reserve the right to refuse any contribution that doesn't align with our mission, values, or operational capabilities.
